Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Protocol Audit: November 2026

1. Scope and Architecture

Auspify Atelier ("the Company", "our platform") engineers precision physical products and digital commerce infrastructure. This Privacy Policy delineates our protocol for the collection, retention, processing, and safeguarding of customer personal data collected via the domain commerce.auspify.internal and associated checkout endpoints.

We adhere to strict data-minimization tenets under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We collect only what is operationally requisite to fulfill manufacturing, order processing, and delivery routing.

2. Data Collection Parameters

Our platform categorizes collected telemetry into two explicit operational registries:

Classification Attributes Collected Operational Justification
Identity & Contact Full Name, Verified Email, Shipping Destination Fulfillment, freight dispatch, invoice issuance
Transaction & Order SKU items, transaction timestamp, payment method token Ledger reconciliation, warranty validation, tax filing
System Telemetry Session identifier, IP address, user agent string Fraud prevention, rate-limiting, cart state preservation

3. Third-Party Disclosures & Freight Partners

Auspify Atelier does not sell, rent, monetize, or barter consumer personal information to advertising brokerages or behavioral tracking syndicates under any circumstance. Data dissemination is restricted exclusively to vetted fulfillment infrastructure:

  • Freight Carriers: Verified commercial logistics partners receive destination addresses and contact phone numbers strictly for package routing.
  • Settlement Gateways: Payment card details are tokenized directly with PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors. Plain-text credit card numbers are never ingested by or persisted in our databases.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosures occur solely when mandated by subpoena, court order, or binding sovereign statutory requirement.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Protocols

Transaction records are retained for seven (7) years to satisfy commercial accounting, corporate tax audit standards, and product liability statutes. Customer session identifiers expire deterministically after 72 hours of inactivity.

Customers maintain the right to request full extraction or permanent cryptographic erasure of their customer account registry by submitting a signed request to compliance@auspify.internal.

5. Cryptographic Security Standards

All client-server communications are enforced via TLS 1.3 encryption. Internal database records utilize salted cryptographic hashing (bcrypt) for user authentication credentials. Access to production data tables is restricted via zero-trust role-based access controls (RBAC).
