INSTITUTIONAL TERMS // SYSTEM USE & GOVERNANCE

TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF SYSTEM USE

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 15, 2026 | VERSION 2.4.0 | JURISDICTION: DELAWARE, USA

01. Acceptance of Terms and Scope of Authorization

Access to and utilization of the Auspify Academic Registry is restricted exclusively to authorized university registrars, departmental administrators, and accredited academic personnel. By accessing this platform, users covenant to comply strictly with these terms and all governing academic honor codes.

02. Credential Security and Operator Accountability

Each operator is assigned individual authentication credentials. Operators must maintain strict confidentiality of their access keys. Any action performed under an operator credential, including record creation, grade alterations, and deletions, is recorded in an immutable audit ledger and attributed directly to that operator account.

03. Prohibited Operational Conduct

Users are strictly prohibited from engaging in any of the following unauthorized activities:

  • Falsifying, inflating, or arbitrarily depressing grade point averages or credit hours.
  • Circumventing data validation rules or injecting unauthorized SQL statements into query parameters.
  • Exporting bulk student registries for non-institutional, personal, or commercial marketing purposes.
  • Sharing administrative sessions or permitting unauthorized third parties to view confidential student dossiers.

04. Data Integrity and Record Retention

The system enforces atomic transactional consistency. While operators retain authority to purge erroneous records in accordance with departmental guidelines, permanent transcript records remain subject to statutory archival requirements. Deletion actions in this portal take immediate effect across active registry indexes.

05. System Availability and SLA

Auspify Technologies targets continuous 99.9% service availability for the registry core. Routine maintenance windows are scheduled during off-peak academic cycles with advance administrative notice. Auspify disclaims liability for transient network disruptions beyond the institutional gateway.

06. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

These terms shall be construed in accordance with the substantive laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflicts of law principles. Any legal proceeding arising under these terms shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located within the state.
