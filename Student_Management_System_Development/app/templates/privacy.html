STUDENT RECORDS PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 15, 2026 | VERSION 2.4.0 | REGULATORY REF: FERPA 34 CFR PART 99

01. Institutional Scope and Regulatory Framework

Auspify Technologies operates this Student Management System on behalf of partner educational institutions. This Privacy Policy governs the lawful collection, processing, retention, and dissemination of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and academic performance records maintained within this database.

All data operations are conducted in strict alignment with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for international scholars, and relevant statutory academic data governance mandates.

02. Categories of Information Collected

The system collects and maintains only records essential for institutional administration, academic advisement, and credential verification:

  • Direct Identifiers: Assigned Institutional Student ID code, legal full name, date of birth, and residency addresses.
  • Institutional Contact Channels: Official university email address, verified telephone numbers, and designated emergency contact records.
  • Academic Performance Records: Departmental affiliation, degree program enrollment, cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA), earned credit hours, course registration histories, and graduation status.
  • Administrative Audit Metadata: Timestamps of record creation, updates, faculty advisor assignments, and formal disciplinary or probation annotations.

03. Permitted Lawful Basis and Processing Purposes

Student data is processed exclusively for the following authorized educational purposes:

  • Administering academic standing, honors recognition, and graduation clearance.
  • Facilitating faculty advisement, course progression tracking, and degree audit workflows.
  • Validating prerequisite eligibility and credit transfer determinations.
  • Generating statutory reports mandated by accreditation bodies and national educational departments.

04. Third-Party Disclosures and Data Integrity

Auspify Technologies does not commercialize, sell, rent, or lease student information to advertisers or commercial brokers. Disclosures are strictly restricted to:

  • Authorized school officials with verified legitimate educational interests.
  • Accredited post-secondary institutions upon formal student transfer authorization.
  • Judicial orders, statutory subpoenas, or health and safety emergencies as expressly defined under FERPA 34 CFR § 99.36.

05. Security Controls and Data Encryption Standards

The database infrastructure utilizes cryptographic safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Access controls enforce role-based permissions, requiring authenticated credentials for academic registrar personnel. System logs capture administrative modifications with immutable timestamps.

06. Student Rights and Access Inquiries

Enrolled scholars retain the statutory right to inspect their academic dossier, contest inaccurate records, and request formal administrative review. Inquiries should be addressed directly to the designated Institutional Data Protection Officer via registrar-compliance@auspify-edu.internal.
