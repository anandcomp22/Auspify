FINANCIAL DATA PRIVACY DISCLOSURE

EFFECTIVE DATE: 2026-01-01 | LAST AUDITED: 2026-11-15 | SECURITY STATUS: ENCRYPTED

01 // INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE AND SCOPE

Auspify Technologies operates the Treasury and Expense Management Platform under strict institutional standards. This policy governs the processing, retention, and isolation of financial records, transaction histories, expense vouchers, and authenticated account identifiers.

Data stored within the system is strictly isolated at the tenant and account layer through relational constraints and authenticated session boundaries.

02 // FINANCIAL RECORD RETENTION & AUDITABILITY

All recorded income and expense transactions maintain an immutable cryptographic reference hash. Modification of records creates an audit timestamp reflecting the exact update event.

Financial records are retained for the operational lifecycle determined by legal reporting requirements. User-initiated deletion completely unlinks and removes associated transaction records from the active ledger.

03 // ACCESS CONTROL & CREDENTIAL ENCRYPTION

User authentication relies on salted cryptographic password hashing via the bcrypt standard. Plaintext passwords are never persisted to storage media or logged to standard output.

API sessions utilize high-entropy cryptographic hex tokens with strictly enforced time-to-live expirations. Tokens transmitted over the wire require TLS encryption under standard HTTPS protocols.

04 // THIRD PARTY DISCLOSURES & INTEGRATIONS

Auspify Technologies does not monetize, sell, distribute, or broker transaction logs or financial patterns to external advertising networks or data brokers.

Exported CSV and JSON statements are generated on-the-fly and streamed directly to the authenticated client without third-party intermediate caching.

05 // DATA GOVERNANCE & CONTACT

For questions regarding data processing or to request ledger compliance certification, contact the treasury systems administration team at security@auspify.internal.
